Jordaan and Canals

sticker
CLAIMED

Houses used to have no numbers, only names and stone pictures on the walls. There are lots of them here. Go find them!

  • 2.1📸
    Three Fleets 🪙×3
    Boats from different companies sail the canals, and the name is written on the side. Take photos of boats from three different companies!
    📍 Piers at Damrak / Prins Hendrikkade
  • 2.2🔍
    Corner Warehouses 🪙×2
    On the warehouses of the Brouwersgracht canal, find the words Vijfhoek, Vierhoek and Driehoek and translate them! And which warehouse is named after a faraway island?
    📍 Brouwersgracht 200–207
  • 2.3🗣️
    Dutch Pie 🪙×3
    At Winkel 43, order apple pie with whipped cream. What did the waiter say? Translate it for us! Then rate the pie from 1 to 10.
    📍 Winkel 43, Noordermarkt 43
  • 2.4🔍
    Three Brothers 🪙×2
    On Bloemgracht there are three brother houses. Their stones show a townsman, a farmer and a sailor. Find the sailor!
    📍 De Drie Hendricken, Bloemgracht 87–91
  • 2.5⚔️
    House of Heads 🪙×2+1
    The legend: a maid caught six thieves, and now their heads are on the wall. Who will be first to find the head with a winged helmet? And the one with ears of wheat?
    📍 Huis met de Hoofden, Keizersgracht 123
👑 Zone boss: Tower Crown 🪙×6
Find the tallest church in Amsterdam. What colour is the crown on top? Wait for the bells to play, then dance until the music stops!
📍 Westerkerk

📜 How to play

  1. We are one team against Amsterdam. 12 zones in any order, 1–2 per outing.
  2. Challenge done? Tick the box ☐ and colour in the coins 🪙. You lead everyone to all the stops with the map and no hints? Another 🧭 +2.
  3. Reached 🏁? The zone is CONQUERED! A sticker on the card and on its number on the big map.
  4. 👑 Boss: a big challenge. It may not work the first time, so try again! ⚔️ Duel: me against the parents. I get the coins for playing either way; the last one (+1) is only for winning. ⏱ Against the clock. 📏 Measures are on the hunt card.
  5. Coins add up over all the outings. Spend them whenever you like: your rank counts everything you have earned!

🌀 City curses

A curse falls when we enter a new zone and the die shows 1, or when we skip a challenge. Roll the die again: the number is the curse. It hits the whole team until we finish the next challenge.

  1. We talk like pirates: "Arr!" at the end of every sentence
  2. We walk like seagulls, arms as wings, until the next bridge
  3. See a boat? Shout "Full steam ahead!"
  4. One hand behind your back
  5. The next challenge on one leg (where you can)
  6. We ring like a tram: "Ding-ding!" at every corner
